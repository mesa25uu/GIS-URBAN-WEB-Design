Project Overview:
Integrated GIS-BIM analysis examining accessibility to schools, healthcare, public transport, and essential services.
Key Datasets (10 layers):

• School routes & accessibility
• Healthcare walking zones
• Public transportation network
• Service distribution points

💡 Tools: ArcGIS JS 4.29
